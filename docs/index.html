The year-end email sequence that outperforms one big appeal.

Most organizations send one email in December and wonder why results are flat. The fix is a five-email arc, and each email has exactly one job.

1) Story — open with a real person your work changed.
2) Impact — show what that change cost and what it produced.
3) Ask — make the direct request, nothing else.
4) Deadline — name the date. Urgency without drama.
5) Last Call — short, plain, sent the morning it ends.

Steal this. Run it. The sequence works because it respects your reader's attention instead of burning it all in one shot.
