Your Google Business profile is free real estate. Most organizations leave it half-empty.

If someone searches for your church, ministry, or business and finds a blank profile, that's not a neutral impression. It's a missed one.

Five fields most organizations skip:

1) Hours (including special or seasonal hours)
2) Description (what you do and who you serve, in plain language)
3) Services or ministry categories
4) Q&A (you can seed your own questions and answer them)
5) A recent post

None of this requires a budget. It requires finishing the work. You already have the profile. Fill it out.
